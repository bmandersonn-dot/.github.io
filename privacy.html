Privacy Policy

Ascension Ministry Planner · Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Ascension Ministry Planner (“the app”) handles Google user data. Back to the home page.

What the app accesses

  • Google Calendar – the app creates, updates and deletes events in a calendar that it creates for itself, so the user's schedule appears on the user's other devices.
  • Google Drive, app-data folder only – the app stores a private, hidden copy of its own data (events, notes, budgets, settings and files the user chooses to add) so the user can use the app on more than one computer. The app cannot see or change any other file in the user's Google Drive.

Where data is stored

The app runs on the user's own computer and keeps its data there. The only other copy is the hidden Drive app-data folder in the user's own Google account. The developer of the app does not operate a server and does not receive, collect or store any of the user's data. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the user's own computer.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising or for training any AI model.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the calendar the app created from Google Calendar, and you can delete the app's hidden Drive data from the same permissions page. Removing the app from your computer deletes the local copy.
